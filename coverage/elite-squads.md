- **Core overlap is total:** B.Fernandes, Calvert-Lewin, João Pedro, Calafiori,
  Mbeumo — all five of the 9+/10 players are owned. The squad's spine IS the
  elite template.
- **The three gaps that matter:** Szoboszlai 8/10 (Anik has Semenyo 1/10),
  Verbruggen 8/10 (Anik has A.Palmer 0/10, non-playing), Maguire 7/10 (Anik
  has Shaw 1/10). Each is a same-position, same-or-cheaper swap.
- **Semenyo → Szoboszlai** is the template-convergence move; the model
  independently rates Szoboszlai 19.0 vs Gakpo 21.6 vs Cherki 20.9 (see
  watchlist.md). Consensus and EV point the same way on selling Semenyo.
- **Gvardiol 0/10** is the one starter no analyst holds. Model has him fine
  (14 pts, form 7.0) — not a signal to sell, but he is a genuine differential.
- **Haaland is a 50/50 among the elite,** not a lock. The Haaland-less half
  run Isak + a stronger midfield/defence.

tally web_name across all 15 picks, rewrite this file. Flag (a) any player
that moved ≥3 seats, (b) captaincy split, (c) chips visible (a 15-active,
